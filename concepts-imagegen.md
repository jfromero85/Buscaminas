# ImageGen Concept Pass

These prompts were used as the concept direction for the visual system before producing final SVG layer assets.

1. **Base movement plate** — "luxury Swiss watch movement foundation plate, brushed rhodium texture, macro product render, dark studio lighting, high detail, centered composition"
2. **Bridge architecture** — "skeleton watch bridge network, beveled polished edges, Côtes de Genève accents, macro premium rendering, isolated"
3. **Gear train** — "mechanical watch transmission gears, gold and steel contrast, precise tooth detail, luxury catalog style"
4. **Balance wheel** — "watch balance wheel and hairspring in motion, elegant blur, warm metal highlights, black background"
5. **Hands and arbor** — "blued steel watch hands, central arbor jewel, modern minimal luxury aesthetic"
6. **Bezel and case ring** — "sapphire-lined bezel ring, engraved minute markers, high-end Swiss watch case details"

Art direction: traditional Swiss finishing language + modern cinematic contrast, midnight navy background, warm champagne-gold accents.
